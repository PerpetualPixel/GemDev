How to play

  • Drag any of the three pieces onto the 8×8 slab. Pieces do not rotate.
  • Fill a whole row or column and it clears. Clear several at once for a bigger payout.
  • Clear again within three placements to keep a streak going. Each step of the streak multiplies your line points.
  • You get three new pieces once all three are placed.
  • The game ends when none of your remaining pieces fits.
